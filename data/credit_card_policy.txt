NEXUS PLATINUM CREDIT CARD - POLICY DOCUMENT

1. CARD LIMITS
- Cash Withdrawal Limit: 20% of the total credit limit.
- International Usage: Enabled by default, can be disabled via app.

2. REWARDS PROGRAM
- 2 Reward Points for every INR 100 spent.
- 5X Reward Points on dining and fuel.
- 1 Reward Point = INR 0.25.

3. FEES
- Annual Fee: INR 499 + GST (Waived on spends > INR 50,000 in previous year).
- Interest Free Period: Up to 48 days.
- Finance Charges: 3.5% per month (42% per annum) on revolving credit.

4. BILLING DISPUTES
- Any dispute regarding a transaction must be raised within 30 days of the statement date.
- Chargeback process will be initiated as per network (Visa/Mastercard) guidelines.

5. LOST/STOLEN CARD
- Liability is NIL for any fraudulent transaction reported after blocking the card.
- Customer is liable for transactions prior to reporting loss, up to a maximum of INR 5000 if not reported immediately.
