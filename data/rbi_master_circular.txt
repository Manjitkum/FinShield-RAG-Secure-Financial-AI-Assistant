RBI MASTER CIRCULAR - 2025-2026
SUBJECT: PRUDENTIAL NORMS FOR FINTECH LENDING

1. INTRODUCTION
This circular sets out the framework for digital lending platforms operating in India. All Regulated Entities (REs) must adhere to these norms to ensure transparency and customer protection.

2. KEY PROVISIONS
2.1. Loan Disbursal: All loan disbursals and repayments must be executed only between the bank accounts of the borrower and the Regulated Entity. No pass-through accounts or pool accounts of third-party lending service providers (LSPs) are permitted.
2.2. Cooling-off Period: A cooling-off/look-up period of at least 3 days must be provided for loans having a tenor of 7 days or more, during which the borrower can exit the loan by paying the principal and the proportionate APR without any penalty.
2.3. Data Privacy: REs shall ensure that any collection of data by their DLAs (Digital Lending Apps) is need-based and with the clear audit trails. Access to mobile phone resources like file and media, contact list, call logs, telephony functions, etc., is prohibited.

3. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
3.1. Nodal Officer: Every RE must appoint a Nodal Grievance Redressal Officer to deal with FinTech/digital lending related complaints.
3.2. Resolution Time: Complaints must be resolved within 30 days. If not, the borrower can complain to the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme of the RBI.

4. REPORTING TO CICs
All lending done through digital lending apps must be reported to Credit Information Companies (CICs) irrespective of the nature/tenor of the loan.

5. PENALTIES
Non-compliance with these directions will invite penal action under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
