NEXUS FINTECH - PERSONAL LOAN TERMS & CONDITIONS (2026)

1. ELIGIBILITY
- Minimum Age: 21 years
- Maximum Age: 60 years at loan maturity
- Minimum Income: INR 25,000 per month
- Credit Score: 700+ (CIBIL)

2. INTEREST RATES & CHARGES
- Annual Percentage Rate (APR): 10.5% to 24% per annum, based on credit profile.
- Processing Fee: Up to 2% of the loan amount + GST.
- Pre-payment Penalty: 
  - Nil if paid after 12 EMIs.
  - 4% of principal outstanding if paid before 12 EMIs.
- Late Payment Charges: 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount.

3. REPAYMENT
- Repayment Mode: NACH/e-Mandate only.
- Tenure: 12 to 60 months.

4. DOCUMENTATION
- KYC: Aadhaar (masked), PAN Card.
- Income Proof: Last 3 months bank statement.

5. DEFAULT
In case of default for more than 90 days, the loan account will be classified as NPA (Non-Performing Asset) and reported to all Credit Information Companies.
